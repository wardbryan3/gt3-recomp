Controller and test limitations

Standard browser-mapped gamepads: bottom face button accelerates, left face button brakes, left stick steers, right shoulder changes camera, left shoulder looks behind, Start pauses. Browser gamepad support may be unavailable over plain HTTP; keyboard controls do not require it. No controller rumble.

Streaming adds capture, encoding, network, browser buffering and decoding latency. Judge practical playability, not local scanout timing. The native renderer retains the expanded full-width view; this transmission is a center crop, not reduced render detail. Known overlay/replay limitations remain.
