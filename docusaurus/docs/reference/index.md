- Generated views: [current products](generated/current-products.md),
  [selected historical inventory](generated/status.md), [symbols](generated/symbols.md),
  [partial layouts](generated/types.md).

Only those four views are regenerated by `just docs-generate`; edit their generator
or appropriate current metadata, not their rendered Markdown alone. Historical
knowledge authorities retain their original names, fingerprints and source hashes.
Later research may qualify an early candidate without rewriting the old record.
