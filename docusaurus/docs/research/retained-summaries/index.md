unique context. They are not regenerated and must not compete with maintained
onboarding, commands or current status. New investigations belong in a focused
topic with explicit scope, not here by default.

## Non-regenerated historical tables

The source directory `registry/` here retains **current-validation, pedal-validation,
pedal-profiles, pedal-closure, handbrake, virtual-pedals and virtual-handbrake**.
“Current” in a historical filename is not today's qualification. These seven files
are snapshots, unlike the four views in [Reference](../../reference/index.md).
The authoritative bounded reports and their original JSON authorities remain in
[native-v3](../checkpoints/native-v3-baseline.md), [pedals](../pedals/index.md) and
[handbrake](../handbrake/index.md). Their source hashes and counts are not repinned.

The retained [donor-source inventory](https://github.com/wardbryan3/gt3-recomp/blob/main/docusaurus/docs/research/provenance/donor-sources.md)
has its existing exclusion too. Use [provenance](../provenance/index.md) for the
maintained explanation. For all ongoing tasks use the single
[current continuation checklist](../../developer-guide/continuation.md).
