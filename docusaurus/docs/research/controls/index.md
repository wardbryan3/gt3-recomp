Read [mapped actions](mapped-actions.md) first, then the [pad trace](pad-steering-trace.md)
and [wheel trace](wheel-steering-trace.md), followed by [steering recovery](steering-recovery.md)
and the [synthetic-wheel experiment](synthetic-wheel.md). These five reports retain
original addresses, units/offsets, private evidence references, independent comparisons
and downstream-boundary limits.

Keep command construction, steering state, rack result and downstream tyre/physics
consumption separate. The bounded clean slice does not recover tyre forces, prove
a physical wheel adapter, or install a product hook. Synthetic equivalence verifies
the chosen protocol path, not actual device range, center, endpoint or live force feedback.

Older steering/synthetic orientation notes are [retained summaries](../retained-summaries/index.md).
Use [current controls architecture](../../architecture/controls.md) and the
[Developer Guide](../../developer-guide/index.md) for maintained instructions.
