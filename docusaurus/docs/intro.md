title: GT3 Recomp knowledge base
---

Recover original GT3 behavior first. Modify or enhance it only under an explicitly
approved contract. This is the single maintained project documentation site;
`project/gt3/` is the machine-readable authority, not another prose tree.

Start with [goals and the three six-month milestones](goals.md),
[build/discovery](getting-started.md), [current architecture](architecture.md),
[source ownership](layout.md), [workflow](workflow.md), [testing](testing.md) and
[restructuring execution status](development/restructuring.md).

Two products are distinct: the permanent enhancement-free `ps2run` reference and
the evolving `gt3-native`. Initially both execute original generated game code;
readable recovered libraries are **not** installed. Native executable output is
not yet the PS2-independent final milestone. Playable and future headless execution
must share gameplay and numerical contracts.

The detailed controls/transmission/extension pages preserve bounded **historical**
findings, original oracles and validation-host results. Their Frozen/validated
labels do not approve current product integration. Current proof, separate approval
and conservative dependency invalidation live in `project/gt3/`; use
[current product ownership](registry/current-products.md), not old counts as a
whole-game completion percentage.

Bring a legal ISO. No game source, assembly/decompiler dumps, tables, generated
translations, states/cards, captures, credentials or game-containing executables
are distributed. See [publication policy](development/PUBLICATION.md).
