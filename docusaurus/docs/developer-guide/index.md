This is the primary path from a public checkout to understanding a bounded GT3
recovery. You can read the complete worked example without proprietary inputs;
executing original-game comparisons requires separately supplied protected inputs.

## Learning sequence

1. **Orient:** check the [environment](environment.md), [source ownership](../reference/repository-layout.md)
   and [build/run workflow](build-and-run.md). Public tests are a useful first contribution.
2. **Plan:** use the [everyday workflow](workflow.md) and [recovery method](recovery-method.md)
   to choose a small observable contract, within explicit authorized scope.
3. **Understand the machine boundary:** learn [representations and ABI](representations.md).
   Read values, widths, bit patterns, mutations and consumers before inventing names.
4. **Work a complete example:** follow [MT selector recovery](worked-example-mt-selector/index.md),
   from original identification through implementation, independent tests and historical live proof.
5. **Interpret proof:** use [validation](validation.md). Compile success, fallback parity,
   actual substitution and physical hardware proof are different claims.
6. **Deliver:** follow [contributing](contributing.md), [publication](publication.md)
   and the [current continuation checklist](continuation.md). Use
   [troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) when a prerequisite or assumption fails.

## What this guide does not authorize

Reading a tutorial or passing a test does not authorize a new recovery, enhancement
or product installation. Current products remain original-generated. Historical
validation-only switches shown in research reports are not supported current game
options. Do not reconstruct retired launchers or enable gates merely to follow a
chapter. Learn the boundary first; obtain separate scope and integration approval.

Keep [Architecture](../architecture/index.md) nearby for the big picture,
[Reference](../reference/index.md) for exact lookup, and
[Research & Evidence](../research/index.md) for source-qualified findings.
