---
title: Enhancement isolation and activation
---

Recovering original behavior and deliberately changing it are different contracts.
An enhancement belongs to the native product only after separate bounded approval,
requires explicit activation, and must preserve original-equivalent defaults when
inactive. Canonical `ps2run` remains enhancement-free.

The historical virtual-transmission experiment changed admission predicates while
retaining original calculations and stores. Choosing an original store under a new
predicate is still **new policy**. Its held-clutch/free behavior is not a discovered
original clutch API. OFF parity and ON policy assertions answer different questions;
ON is not expected to reproduce the unchanged original history.

Keep domain policy separate from concrete execution bindings and reject unsupported
combinations rather than assuming hooks compose. Current gate-OFF/ON syntax checks
are not enabled gameplay proof. The historical isolated runner is non-authoritative
and not a supported current product option.

See the [evaluated enhancement](../research/enhancements/virtual-transmission-v1.md)
and its [preceding stopped audit](../research/enhancements/stopped-boundary-audit.md).
Neither their historical permissions nor this architecture description approves
installation, hardware adapters, physics retunes or another recovery.
